Noobtales Publisher

Roblox stories. Always a twist.

Noobtales Publisher is a small private tool that uploads finished Noobtales videos (animated Roblox story shorts) to the Noobtales channels on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok.

It is used only by the channel's owner, to post the channel's own videos. It is not a public service: there is no sign-up, and it does not access anyone else's account.
