Privacy Policy

Noobtales Publisher · effective 26 November 2026

Noobtales Publisher ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the Noobtales channels to upload the channel's own videos to YouTube, Instagram and TikTok.

What the app accesses

When the owner signs in, the app receives permission to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and to read that channel's name (to confirm the right account), and permission to publish videos to the owner's own Instagram and TikTok accounts. It uses this access only to upload videos the owner has chosen to publish.

What the app stores

The sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. The app has no server and keeps no database. It collects no information from viewers, followers or anyone else.

What the app shares

Nothing. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Videos and captions are sent only to the platform they are being published on.

Google user data

Noobtales Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time: for Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, for Instagram under Facebook's Settings › Business integrations, and for TikTok under Settings › Security › Manage app permissions. Deleting the app's local token file removes everything the app stores.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Noobtales YouTube channel.
